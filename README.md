VR Ruwanwelisaya 🇱🇰

Exploring Sri Lankan Heritage Through Virtual Reality

VR Ruwanwelisaya is an immersive Virtual Reality project designed to digitally recreate and showcase the historic Ruwanwelisaya Stupa in Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka.

The project aims to provide users with an engaging virtual experience where they can explore one of Sri Lanka's most significant cultural and historical landmarks through VR technology.

By combining immersive technology with cultural heritage, this project explores new possibilities for making historical locations more accessible, interactive, and engaging.

🎯 Project Objectives

Digitally recreate the Ruwanwelisaya environment.

Provide an immersive virtual exploration experience.

Promote awareness of Sri Lankan cultural and historical heritage.

Explore the use of VR technology in education and tourism.

Demonstrate the potential of digital technology in cultural preservation.

🌍 Project Vision

Our vision is to bridge the gap between technology and heritage by creating immersive digital experiences that allow people to explore and appreciate Sri Lanka's historical landmarks in new ways.
